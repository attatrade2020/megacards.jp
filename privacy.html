Privacy Policy

How MegaCards.jp uses customer information to provide accounts, orders, payment links, delivery and support.

On this pageInformationUseSharingSecurityYour choicesContact

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating or editing an account, requesting a password reset, placing an order or contacting support. This can include your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, encrypted password, order contents, delivery selection, payment method and support messages.

We also collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser information, security logs and analytics events needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How information is used

  • Create and maintain member accounts.
  • Validate prices and stock, reserve products and process orders.
  • Create Wise or PayPal payment links and reconcile payment status.
  • Calculate DHL or FedEx shipping and deliver orders.
  • Send order, payment and shipping messages.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Measure storefront performance with Google Analytics 4.

3. Service providers and international processing

Information is shared only as needed with service providers involved in the transaction, including Wise or PayPal for payment, DHL or FedEx and shipping technology providers for delivery, email infrastructure for customer messages, Google for analytics, and hosting providers for operation and security. Information can be processed in Japan, Australia and other countries where these providers operate.

MegaCards does not sell customer personal information.

4. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies keep member and administrator sessions secure. Google Analytics 4 helps measure page use and shopping steps. Browser settings and the Google Analytics opt-out tool can be used to limit analytics collection.

5. Security and retention

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Administrative functions use authenticated sessions, and order links use time-limited authorization tokens. Payment credentials are stored outside the public website directory. Personal information is retained for as long as required to fulfil orders, provide support, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service.

6. Access and correction

Members can edit their name, email, phone number and shipping or billing address from My Account. To request access, correction or deletion of other personal information, email MegaCards. Some order and transaction information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.

7. Children

The store is intended for customers able to enter into a purchase contract. MegaCards does not knowingly create accounts for children who cannot legally consent to the collection of their information.

8. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to info@megacards.jp. Include enough information for MegaCards to identify the relevant account or order.
