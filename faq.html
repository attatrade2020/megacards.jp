01 · Ordering

Buying from Japan

Do you ship to all Australian states and territories?

Yes. We ship from Japan to addresses across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, and NT. Carrier availability and price depend on the destination postcode, parcel size, and weight.

How do I create a MegaCards account?

Select Create account in the site header or hero banner, then enter your name, email, state or territory, and a password. Passwords are securely hashed before storage and are never saved as readable text.

Are prices shown in Australian dollars?

Product and shipping estimates are displayed in Australian dollars (AUD) for easy comparison. Payment is supported in both AUD and Japanese yen (JPY), depending on the selected payment flow. The applicable currency, amount, and any conversion are shown before you confirm payment.

Which payment methods and currencies can I use?

You can pay through Wise or PayPal in Australian dollars (AUD) or Japanese yen (JPY); payment is not limited to JPY. Add your boxes, calculate shipping, and choose your payment method at checkout. Wise or PayPal will show the payment currency, amount, exchange rate, and any conversion before you confirm.

PayPal orders include a clearly itemised 5% transaction fee. After submitting the order, you will receive a secure payment link on screen and by email. Payment links are valid for 10 minutes. Once payment has been successfully completed, the order is final and cannot be cancelled.

What products can I order?

MegaCards currently sells sealed boxes only. The catalogue includes Japanese Pokémon booster boxes and special boxes, plus ONE PIECE Card Game and Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG boxes.

Can I order more than one box?

Yes. Add each box to the cart. The shipping calculator combines the saved weight and dimensions of every box, adds protective packaging, and requests rates for the resulting parcel.

02 · Shipping & delivery

Delivery from Japan

Where will my order be shipped from?

Every MegaCards order is packed and dispatched from Japan directly to the Australian delivery address entered at checkout. Add at least one box to the cart, enter your four-digit Australian postcode, and select your state to see available tracked international services and prices in AUD.

Which carriers do you use?

Shipping quotes are limited to DHL and FedEx tracked international services. Only DHL or FedEx services currently available for the parcel and destination are displayed.

Are shipments tracked?

Yes. Orders are sent using tracked international services. Tracking details are provided after dispatch and may take a short time to begin showing scans.

How long does delivery take?

DHL and FedEx express services commonly arrive within several business days after dispatch. Carrier delays, customs processing, remote-area delivery, and peak periods can affect timing. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

How are boxes packed?

Boxes are packed with protective material inside a suitably sized outer carton. The shipping quote includes each product’s saved dimensions and weight plus an allowance for the outer carton and packing material.

Can you deliver to a PO Box?

DHL and FedEx generally require a physical street address for international courier delivery. Enter a residential or business street address at checkout rather than a PO Box.

03 · GST & customs

Australian import costs

Is GST included in the displayed price?

GST is not included unless it is expressly shown during checkout or on the invoice. Australian GST rules for low-value imported goods can require GST to be collected at the point of sale in some circumstances.

Will Australian Border Force charge me when the order arrives?

For most consignments valued at AUD 1,000 or less, Australian Border Force says there are generally no duties, taxes, or charges collected at the border, although GST may be collected at the point of sale. A consignment valued above AUD 1,000 generally requires an Import Declaration and can attract duties, taxes, and processing charges.

Read the Australian Border Force buying-online guidance.

Can the Japan–Australia trade agreement reduce customs duty?

Possibly. The Japan–Australia Economic Partnership Agreement (JAEPA) provides preferential tariff treatment for qualifying Japanese-origin goods. A product must meet the applicable tariff classification and rules of origin, and suitable origin documentation may be required. Buying an item in Japan or selling a Japanese edition does not by itself establish eligibility.

For orders above AUD 1,000, the importer or customs broker should confirm the classification, origin status, documentation, and applicable rate before claiming the preference. Read the Australian Government JAEPA guide.

Are customs charges included in the shipping quote?

No. The shipping calculator shows the carrier charge only. Any GST, customs duty, import declaration fee, brokerage fee, or other government charge is separate unless specifically stated.

Who is responsible for import clearance?

The recipient is the importer and is responsible for providing information and paying charges requested by the carrier or Australian authorities. For orders above AUD 1,000, consider using a licensed customs broker.

05 · Orders & support

After you order

What should I do if my parcel arrives damaged?

Photograph the unopened outer carton, shipping label, internal packaging, and affected product before discarding anything. Contact info@megacards.jp within five days of delivery so we can review the shipment and carrier claim options.

Can I return an order?

Contact us within five days if a box is incorrect, damaged, or significantly different from its description. Returns are not a cancellation option after payment, and change-of-mind returns are not accepted. Your rights under applicable consumer law remain unaffected.

Can I cancel an order?

Contact us before completing payment if you need help with an order. Once payment has been successfully completed, the order is final and cannot be cancelled. This cancellation policy does not limit any rights or remedies that apply when a product is incorrect, damaged, or significantly different from its description.

How do I contact MegaCards?

Email info@megacards.jp with your order number, full name, and a clear description of the issue. Include photographs when asking about damage or product condition.
