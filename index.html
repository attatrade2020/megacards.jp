FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? ▼
We ship from Nagoya, Japan. UPS Saver typically takes 3-5 business days to North America and Europe. FedEx/DHL Express is 2-4 days. Exact rates and delivery times are shown in your cart at checkout.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept Wise (TransferWise) and PayPal. Wise transfers have no processing fee. PayPal orders include a 5% processing fee on the total (subtotal + shipping). You can also buy directly through our eBay store (phoenix-japan) with standard eBay payment options.
What does "Sealed" vs "No Shrink" mean? ▼
"Sealed" means the box has its original factory shrinkwrap intact — the most desirable condition for collectors. "No Shrink" means the box is complete and unopened, but the original shrinkwrap has been removed (common for older Japanese products). Both are new and unplayed.
What is your return policy? ▼
Returns are accepted within 5 days of delivery for items in original condition. Cancellations before shipment are subject to a 30% restocking fee. Once shipped, orders cannot be cancelled. Please see our full Terms of Service for details.
Do you offer bulk discounts? ▼
Yes! We offer quantity-based pricing for orders of 3+ boxes. The more you buy, the better the per-unit price. Contact us with your desired products and quantities for a custom quote.
